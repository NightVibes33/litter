Privacy Policy

Alley Cåt

How the app handles your connections, conversations, files, and diagnostics.

Effective date: October 7, 2026

Support

Who to contact

Alley Cåt is maintained by NightVibes33. For privacy questions or requests, email NightVibes33@proton.me.

Data stored on your device

The app stores settings, saved connections, account credentials or authentication tokens, and local runtime or workspace data needed for the features you use. Credentials use platform-provided protected storage where implemented. Conversation and workspace data may also be stored by your connected runtime.

Connections and AI services

When you connect to an AI provider, computer, SSH host, or runtime, the app sends the requests and content needed for that connection. This may include messages, selected attachments, workspace information, and commands you approve or authorize. Authentication exchanges send the information needed to sign in to the selected service.

Those services handle data under their own privacy policies and retention rules. Using a local runtime does not guarantee that its AI requests stay on your device. Review the provider and runtime settings before sharing sensitive information.

Voice, images, and files

If you use voice features, microphone audio is processed by the configured voice service. Selected images and files may be sent to your connected runtime or provider. The app requests device permissions for features that need them; you can manage permissions in system settings.

Diagnostics and support

The app keeps bounded local session logs and may save system diagnostic reports to help investigate crashes. Logs are redacted to reduce exposure of sensitive values, but redaction cannot guarantee removal of every sensitive detail. Inspect reports before sharing them.

If you email support or submit a bug report, the maintainer receives the information you provide and uses it to respond and investigate. Public GitHub issues are visible to others; do not post credentials, private conversations, or unreviewed diagnostic reports. Apple may separately process TestFlight feedback and crash information under its policies.

Advertising and tracking

The app does not include an advertising network or use an advertising identifier to track you across other companies’ apps and websites. Connected third-party services have their own practices.

Removal and retention

You can remove saved connections and sign out where supported. Deleting the app removes its app-container data, but protected credentials, device backups, exported files, and copies held by connected services may remain. Revoke provider tokens and delete remote data with the relevant service when needed.

To request removal of information you sent directly to support, email the address above. Data held by your AI provider or connected host must be requested from that service or administrator.

Policy updates

This page may be updated as the app changes. The effective date above identifies the current policy. This policy describes the app and does not replace third-party service policies.
